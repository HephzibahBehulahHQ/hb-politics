## About HB Politics

**HB Politics** is the governance, civic education, public policy and political research division of **Hephzibah Behulah**.

We develop digital platforms, educational resources, research tools and data-driven applications that help individuals understand political institutions, democratic processes, government policies and civic responsibilities.

Our mission is to make political information more accessible, understandable, factual and useful to the public.

We believe informed citizens are better equipped to understand government decisions, participate responsibly in civic life and evaluate public policies.

---

## Our Vision

To build a trusted digital ecosystem that promotes civic knowledge, responsible public discourse, government transparency and accessible political education.

## Our Mission

To provide clear, evidence-based political information and educational technology that helps people understand governance, public institutions and policies affecting their communities.

## Editorial and Research Standards

HB Politics is committed to responsible public-interest information.

Our publishing principles include:

1. Verify factual claims before publication.
2. Prioritize official documents and primary sources.
3. Distinguish verified facts from opinions and analysis.
4. Clearly identify publication dates and relevant updates.
5. Correct material errors transparently.
6. Avoid misleading headlines, manipulated evidence and unsupported allegations.
7. Provide appropriate context for political developments.
8. Respect privacy, copyright and applicable laws.
9. Present relevant perspectives fairly.
10. Avoid partisan advocacy in factual reporting and civic education.

## Political Independence and Transparency

HB Politics aims to provide educational and research resources grounded in verifiable information.

Factual reporting and civic education should not promote a political party or candidate.

Opinion and commentary, where published, must be clearly distinguished from factual reporting.

Political information may change over time. Important claims should include relevant dates and sources.
