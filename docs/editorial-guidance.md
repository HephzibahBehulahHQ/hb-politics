# Civic information and editorial guidance

- Prefer official documents and primary sources; retain publication dates and source context.
- Distinguish verified facts, analysis, opinion and commentary.
- Correct material factual errors transparently and avoid unsupported allegations.
- Do not present political information as current without checking dates and sources.
- Maintain factual civic education without partisan advocacy in factual reporting.
- Keep jurisdiction-specific legal, election and data requirements in the responsible product or editorial workflow.
